        Under the hood

        How it works

        Six steps between your question and the answer.

        1 · Safety first

        Emergencies such as chest pain happening right now or self-harm get fixed guidance (112, 108, Tele-MANAS 14416) straight away, with no AI involved. Dose, personal and "latest" questions are never answered from the cache.

        2 · CAG: verified cache

        A reworded question gets a verified earlier answer in milliseconds, but only if its meaning, intent and clinical context all match.

        3 · RAG: retrieval

        Otherwise, MiniLM embeddings find the 5 most relevant passages among 27,470 from NIH websites (MedQuAD).

        4 · Evidence gate

        If nothing relevant is found, the system says so instead of guessing.

        5 · Lightweight LLM

        A small, fast model writes a short answer using only those passages, and cites each one.

        6 · Verify, then remember

        Every sentence is checked against the passage it cites. Unsupported answers are replaced by the sources themselves. Only verified, reusable answers go into the shared cache.

        Why the cache needs a gate

        Two questions can look alike and still need different answers.

        "Symptoms of anemia" and "symptoms of anemia in children" are more than 90% similar to an embedding model. A plain cache would reuse the wrong answer. MedRAC-QA checks intent, age group, pregnancy, type numbers and topic words first.

        cachedWhat are the symptoms of anemia?
        reusedWhat signs and symptoms does anemia cause?
        not reusedWhat are the symptoms of anemia in children?
